Project Overview

This end-to-end Business Intelligence project transforms raw AdventureWorks data into actionable business insights. Using SQL and DuckDB, the data was cleaned, transformed, and modelled into a Star Schema optimised for analytics. The model was then imported into Power BI, where DAX measures and calculated columns power a suite of interactive dashboards covering sales performance, product analytics, and customer behaviour.

The solution provides stakeholders with real-time visibility into key metrics, enabling data-driven decision making across the organisation.

Analytics Workflow

AdventureWorks Dataset

Sourced from the official AdventureWorks sample database, containing sales, product, customer, and territory data.

SQL Data Cleaning (DuckDB)

Applied SQL transformations in DuckDB to clean nulls, standardise formats, remove duplicates, and prepare data for modelling.

Star Schema Modeling

Designed a Star Schema with Fact tables and Dimension tables to optimise query performance and enable intuitive analysis.

Power BI Development

Imported the Star Schema into Power BI and built an interactive multi-page report with slicers, filters, and drill-through functionality.

DAX Measures

Created 15+ DAX measures including total revenue, profit margins, year-over-year growth, and customer lifetime value.

Interactive Dashboard

Delivered a fully interactive dashboard with four pages: Sales, Product Performance, Customer & Territory, and Data Model documentation.

Key Business Insights

Bikes generate the majority of revenue across all product categories.

Mountain and Road bike models are the strongest revenue drivers.

Professionals contribute the highest customer revenue.

United States and Australia are the highest-performing markets.

Revenue is distributed almost evenly between male and female customers.

Product return rate remains low, indicating strong product-market fit.
